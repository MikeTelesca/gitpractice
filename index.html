CSV Format Help

Your CSV should have columns for Date, Description, and Amount. The parser auto-detects most formats:

Date,Description,Amount
2024-01-15,SHOPPERS DRUG MART,-45.67
2024-01-16,PAYROLL DEPOSIT,3200.00

Negative = expense, Positive = income. Debit/Credit column formats also supported.
